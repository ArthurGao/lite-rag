# Northwind Labs councils (fictional example)

Councils decide questions that cross teams. Each section says when the council meets and what it approves.

## Data Safety Council

The Data Safety Council meets every second Thursday. It approves any new use of customer data.

## Spending Council

The Spending Council meets on the first Monday of each month. It approves purchases above 20,000 euros.

## Hiring Council

The Hiring Council meets every Wednesday afternoon. It approves every offer to a senior candidate.

## Architecture Council

The Architecture Council meets on the last Friday of each month. It approves new programming languages and databases.
