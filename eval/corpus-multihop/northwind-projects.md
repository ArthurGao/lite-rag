# Northwind Labs projects (fictional example)

Northwind Labs runs four internal projects. Each section names the project's goal and its lead.

## Project Atlas

Project Atlas builds the new billing system that replaces the old invoicing scripts. Its lead is Maria Keller.

## Project Beacon

Project Beacon builds the mobile app for field engineers, with offline maps. Its lead is Tom Osei.

## Project Cedar

Project Cedar moves the data warehouse to a columnar database. Its lead is Priya Nair.

## Project Delta

Project Delta rebuilds the customer support portal and its search. Its lead is Jonas Berg.
