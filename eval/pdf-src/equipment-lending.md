# Equipment Lending Policy

The media lab lends equipment to staff and students. Each kind of equipment has its own loan period, late fee and damage rule, so check the section for what you borrow. Loans are booked at the front desk with a valid card.

## Laptops

### Loan period

A loan runs for fourteen days and can be renewed once if nobody is waiting. Renewals are made at the front desk before the due date.

### Late fees

Each day past the due date costs two dollars, up to a maximum of forty dollars per loan.

### Damage

The borrower pays for repairs up to two hundred dollars. Damage above that amount is covered by the lab's insurance.

## Cameras

### Loan period

A loan runs for three days, including weekends, and cannot be renewed. Lenses and batteries go out with the body and come back together.

### Late fees

Each day past the due date costs ten dollars, with no maximum, because cameras are in short supply.

### Damage

The borrower pays the full repair cost. A lost lens cap or strap is charged at the replacement price.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## Projectors

### Loan period

A loan runs for one day and must be returned by nine the next morning. Weekend loans run from Friday noon to Monday nine.

### Late fees

Each hour past the due time costs five dollars, because the next booking usually starts at nine.

### Damage

The borrower pays for a broken lamp. Any other damage is reviewed by the lab manager before a charge is set.
