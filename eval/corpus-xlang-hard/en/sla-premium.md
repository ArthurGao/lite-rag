# Harbourline Cloud Premium service level agreement (fictional example)

## Availability

The Premium tier commits to 99.9% monthly availability of the control plane.

## Service credits

If Premium availability falls below the commitment, the customer receives a credit of 25% of that month's fee.

## Support

A Premium severity-one ticket receives a first human response within two hours.

## Maintenance

Planned maintenance for Premium customers takes place on Sunday 03:00 to 05:00 (UTC).
