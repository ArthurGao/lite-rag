# Harbourline Cloud Enterprise service level agreement (fictional example)

## Availability

The Enterprise tier commits to 99.95% monthly availability of the control plane.

## Service credits

If Enterprise availability falls below the commitment, the customer receives a credit of 50% of that month's fee.

## Support

A Enterprise severity-one ticket receives a first human response within thirty minutes.

## Maintenance

Planned maintenance for Enterprise customers takes place on the first Tuesday of each month, 01:00 to 02:00 (UTC).
