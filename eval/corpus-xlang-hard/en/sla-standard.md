# Harbourline Cloud Standard service level agreement (fictional example)

## Availability

The Standard tier commits to 99.5% monthly availability of the control plane.

## Service credits

If Standard availability falls below the commitment, the customer receives a credit of 10% of that month's fee.

## Support

A Standard severity-one ticket receives a first human response within eight business hours.

## Maintenance

Planned maintenance for Standard customers takes place on Sunday 02:00 to 06:00 (UTC).
