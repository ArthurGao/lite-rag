# A short guide to the planets

## The inner planets

Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars are rocky planets close to the Sun. Mercury is the smallest planet and has almost no atmosphere, so its temperature swings between extreme heat and cold.

## Venus

Venus is the hottest planet, even though Mercury is closer to the Sun. Its thick carbon dioxide atmosphere traps heat in a runaway greenhouse effect, keeping the surface around 465 degrees Celsius.

## Mars

Mars looks red because iron oxide, rust, covers much of its surface. It has the tallest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons, about three times the height of Mount Everest.

## The gas giants

Jupiter and Saturn are made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Jupiter is the largest planet; its Great Red Spot is a storm wider than Earth that has lasted for centuries.

## The ice giants

Uranus and Neptune contain more water, ammonia and methane ices. Uranus rotates on its side, with an axis tilted about 98 degrees, probably after an ancient collision.
